Why JMLS Exists

Growing companies reach a moment where historical accounting is no longer enough.

As revenue scales and operations expand, the financial questions keeping founders and CEOs awake at night shift rapidly. You are no longer just looking backward to reconcile last month’s ledger or file year-end tax returns—you are allocating capital, managing working-capital pressure, negotiating with lenders, and evaluating margin trade-offs in real time.

Yet for many ambitious enterprises, hiring a full-time, seasoned VP of Finance or Chief Financial Officer is premature. That gap often leaves leadership teams trapped in three familiar friction points:

01 · Visibility Gap

Deciding Without Forward Cash Clarity

Making high-stakes hiring, inventory, or capital commitments based on static, backward-looking financial statements that arrive weeks after month-end.

02 · Structural Strain

Outgrowing Internal Controls & Processes

Scaling transaction volume, supply chains, or multi-entity operations faster than your financial governance—creating margin leakage and operational risk.

03 · Capital & Board Pressure

Navigating Inflection Points Alone

Preparing for bank facilities, investor scrutiny, audits, acquisitions, or ownership transitions without defensible models or an experienced financial voice at the table.

Insights & Clarity

Your Questions, Answered

What exactly does a Fractional CFO do compared to our bookkeeper or external tax CPA?
Your bookkeeper records day-to-day historical transactions, and your external public accounting firm focuses on year-end financial statements and tax compliance. A Fractional CFO looks forward—owning financial strategy, cash flow forecasting, margin optimization, internal controls, lender/board reporting, and executive decision support. We don’t replace your bookkeeper or tax CPA; we bridge the gap between them and the CEO.
How do we know when our company is ready for a Fractional CFO?
Most companies reach this inflection point when growth creates complexity: cash flow feels unpredictable despite strong sales, month-end reports arrive too late or lack actionable insight, lenders or investors are asking for deeper forecasts, or the founder is spending too many evenings wrestling with spreadsheets instead of leading the business.
How is a JMLS engagement structured and billed?
Fractional engagements are typically structured as a predictable monthly retainer based on the cadence your business requires (for example, a set number of days or hours per month), with no hidden overhead, benefits, or equity dilution. For diagnostic sprints or special projects, we offer clear fixed-scope or milestone-based arrangements.
What industries does John Scalise and JMLS Consulting specialize in?
John brings 20+ years of senior leadership across oil & gas / energy services, specialty chemical manufacturing, data-center & AI cooling technologies, industrial & rail logistics, and high-growth luxury e-commerce / retail platforms. Because core financial architecture—cash velocity, unit economics, internal controls, and risk management—transcends sector boundaries, we adapt rapidly to complex B2B and B2C operating models.
Where is JMLS Consulting based, and do you work with clients across Canada?
JMLS Consulting Inc. is headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, and serves clients Canada-wide. We work seamlessly with remote and hybrid leadership teams across all Canadian provinces, combining regular virtual executive cadences with on-site working sessions for key board meetings, strategy offsites, or operational reviews.
How involved is a Fractional CFO in our day-to-day operations?
We embed as a practical, hands-on member of your leadership team. Rather than dropping off a monthly report and disappearing, we participate in leadership meetings, review variances directly with department heads, mentor your internal accounting staff, and stay accessible when urgent commercial or pricing decisions arise.
Why is internal control and Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) so important for growing firms?
When companies scale rapidly, informal approval habits and spreadsheet workarounds often leave the business exposed to cash leakage, inventory discrepancies, fraud risk, or audit failures. Drawing on over a decade of VP Audit & Risk leadership, we implement right-sized controls that protect your balance sheet without adding red tape.
Can you work alongside our existing Controller or internal finance team?
Absolutely. Many of our clients have a capable Controller or Senior Accountant who manages daily accounting well but hasn’t led corporate forecasting, banking negotiations, ERM, or board-level strategy. We provide executive direction and mentorship that makes your existing team stronger and more confident.
What happens during the first 30 to 60 days of an engagement?
We start by stabilizing visibility: reviewing your historical financials, close process, cash runway, and key risk exposures. Within the first 30–60 days, we typically establish a reliable 13-week cash flow forecast, clean up key reporting bottlenecks, and align with the CEO on the top three financial priorities for the next two quarters.
Can our engagement scale up or down as our business needs change?
Yes. One of the greatest advantages of fractional leadership is elasticity. We can increase our involvement during a financing round, acquisition, audit, or system rollout, and scale back to a steady maintenance cadence once the milestone is complete—and when you are ready for a full-time CFO, we help recruit and transition them seamlessly.
Do you assist with bank financing, covenant management, and investor due diligence?
Yes. We prepare lender-ready financial models, manage due diligence data rooms, monitor debt covenants proactively, and sit beside you in meetings with commercial bankers, private equity groups, or prospective buyers so your financial story is credible and stress-tested.
